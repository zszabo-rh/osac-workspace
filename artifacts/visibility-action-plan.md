## Phase 2: Meeting Participation (Weeks 3-4, Aug 25 - Sep 5)

### Preparation Routine (before each Tuesday storage meeting)

1. Read agenda (posted in wg-osac-storage)
2. Pick ONE topic to contribute on
3. Write down the exact sentence to say
4. Have it ready — read it if needed, nobody will notice

### Safe contribution patterns

- "Can someone clarify how X relates to Y?"
- "I was looking at [code/PR] and noticed [thing] — is that intentional?"
- "I agree with [person] because [specific reason]"
- "When I was working on OSAC-3234 I ran into [relevant experience]"
- "[This approach] reminds me of [thing from past experience] — same tradeoff?"
